### Top 5 Critical Issues

1. No schema markup anywhere — 0 structured data blocks across 23 pages. This alone suppresses rich results, AI citations, and knowledge panel eligibility.
2. Missing robots.txt and sitemap.xml — search engines have no crawling guidance or URL discovery path.
3. No canonical URLs on any page — when deployed, duplicate content risk from www/non-www, HTTP/HTTPS, and URL variants.
4. 14 of 21 content pages are below 300 words — thin content at scale will trigger Google quality penalties.
5. Zero E-E-A-T signals — no author names, no about page, no citations, no publication dates.

### Top 5 Quick Wins

1. Add FAQPage schema to homepage FAQ section — immediate rich-result eligibility.
2. Add Organization + WebSite schema to homepage — table-stakes brand signal.
3. Create robots.txt and XML sitemap — foundational crawlability.
4. Add Open Graph tags to BaseLayout — fixes social sharing for all pages.
5. Fix garbled UTF-8 characters in /dynasties/ and /accessories/ meta descriptions.
